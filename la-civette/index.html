About La Civette

Where the humidor meets the espresso machine.

Tucked into the streets near Rue Bonaparte and Rue de Seine, La Civette is a classic Parisian tabac with a twist: alongside the cigarettes, rolling tobacco, and a well-kept selection of cigars, there's a small coffee counter that's become the real reason people stop in.

It's the kind of shop that rewards a second visit — ask what's good in the humidor that week, or just come for the espresso and stay for the conversation.
