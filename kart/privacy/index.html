Kart Cup Privacy Policy

Last updated November 17, 2026. Kart Cup is made by L and H LLC.

Kart Cup is a tool for running a kart racing tournament with friends. It has no user accounts, no ads, and we do not sell or share personal information.

What we store

  • Names you enter. Racer names typed by the host, or entered by players on the sign-up page, are stored with the tournament.
  • Tournament results. Which games each racer played, who advanced, the winner, the optional cup played, and the tournament's name and date.

This information is stored on our hosting provider, Netlify, so the host can save tournaments and others can follow along. Anyone who opens the app can see saved tournaments, their racer names and results, and the leaderboard. Please only enter a first name or nickname.

What stays on your device

  • The host's passcode and a host sign-in token, so the host stays signed in.
  • A copy of the tournament in progress and the setup form, so nothing is lost if the page reloads.
  • On the sign-up page, the names entered from that device.

Clearing your browser or app data removes these.

Analytics

The app uses Google Analytics to count visits and see how pages are used. Google Analytics collects information such as your device type, browser, approximate location from your IP address, and pages viewed. See Google's Privacy Policy for how Google handles this data.

Payments

The "Support it on Venmo" link opens Venmo. Kart Cup does not process payments or see payment details; Venmo's own privacy policy applies there.

Children

Kart Cup is meant to be run by an adult host. It does not knowingly collect information from children beyond the first name or nickname a host enters for a racer.

Removing your information

To have a tournament or a racer's name removed, ask the host, who can delete tournaments in the app, or contact us through landhllc.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
