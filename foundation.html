Why this exists

Kids shouldn't have to worry about meals or medicine.

Somewhere near you, right now, there's a kid going without something basic — a real meal, a trip to the doctor, the peace of mind that comes from not worrying about either. That shouldn't be normal, and it shouldn't require a mountain of paperwork to fix.

I started the Luke Higgins Foundation because I wanted a simple, direct way to help. This isn't a big institution with overhead and committees — it's one person putting real contributions toward real needs: food, insurance costs, and the everyday essentials that fall through the cracks for families who are already stretched thin.

Every dollar given here goes toward that, as directly as I can make it happen.

Being upfront

This is a personal giving effort, not a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit — contributions here are not tax-deductible. Gifts go directly to Luke Higgins, who puts them toward helping children access food and basic healthcare needs. Venmo doesn't support automatic recurring gifts on personal profiles, so "$10 a month" means exactly that: sending $10 each month yourself. A calendar reminder makes it easy to keep it going.
